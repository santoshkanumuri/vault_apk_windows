5. Your master password and backups

Nuvori is built so that only your master password can open your vault. The developer has no copy of it and no way to reset it. If you forget it and have no working backup or other unlocked device, your data cannot be recovered.

  • Choose a strong master password and keep it safe.
  • Keep your devices protected with a screen lock and current updates.
  • Make backups: export your vault, turn on Google Drive backup on Android, or keep a second paired device.
  • Before you change a password on a website, make sure the new one was saved in Nuvori and accepted by the site.

You are responsible for the security of your devices, your master password and your backups.
