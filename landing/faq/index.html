Getting started

Is Nuvori free?

Yes. Nuvori costs nothing. It has no ads, no in-app purchases and no Nuvori account.

Google Drive sync and Google Drive backup are optional. They use storage in your own Google account.

Which devices can I use, and how many?
  • Android phones and tablets with Android 10 or newer.
  • Wear OS 3 or newer watches, together with the phone app. The watch shows your authenticator codes.
  • Windows 10 (version 2004) or newer, 64-bit.
  • The browser extension for browsers built on Chromium or Firefox, such as Zen, Helium, Comet, Arc, Brave, Vivaldi, Firefox, Chrome and Edge. It works together with Nuvori for Windows. See Which browsers work?

One vault can have up to 6 phones or tablets and 2 Windows PCs. Passkeys need Android 14 or newer, or Windows 11.

There is no Nuvori app for iPhone, iPad or Mac.

What does sync copy between my devices?

Sync copies your vault to every device in your sync group:

  • Logins, cards, authenticator codes, notes and security questions.
  • Autofill details: names, emails, phone numbers and addresses.
  • Passkeys and photos.
  • Folders, device names, and your accent and background colors.
  • Website icons that Nuvori for Windows saves from your browser.

Some things stay on each device: most settings, Recently deleted, Previous passwords, passkey history and the activity log.

Each device keeps its own full copy. It works offline and catches up the next time it connects.

Do all my devices need the same master password?

Phones and tablets: yes. All Android devices in one vault use the same master password. Pairing checks this before it copies anything.

If the new device's empty vault has another password, choose Change this empty vault's password on the pairing screen.

Windows PCs: no. A PC has its own master password, and it can differ from your phone's. During pairing, you type your phone's master password once on the PC.

While other devices share your vault, the Change master password button on Android stays off.

I forgot my master password. Can you reset it?

No. Nobody can reset or recover it, including the developer. There is no Nuvori server with a copy. Backups also open only with a master password.

What you can still do:

  • Another device still unlocks: your items are safe there. Set up the forgotten device again as a new, empty device, and pair it. For a PC, see I reinstalled Windows or got a new PC. For a phone, see I lost my phone, but my PC has my vault.
  • No device unlocks: the vault can't be opened. Start a new vault. On Android, uninstall and reinstall Nuvori. This deletes the old vault on that phone.

Keep a copy of your master password somewhere safe, for example on paper at home.

Switching browsers

I'm switching browsers. What happens to my passwords?

Nothing. Your logins, cards, addresses and codes stay in Nuvori for Windows, not in the browser.

  1. Add Nuvori Password Fill to the new browser, from the Chrome Web Store or Firefox Add-ons.
  2. Nuvori finds the browser and connects it. Restart the browser once.
  3. Unlock Nuvori. The same logins fill in the new browser.

You can keep using the old browser too. Check its connection in Settings > Browser extension.

Do I need to import my passwords again when I change browsers?

No. You move your passwords into Nuvori once. After that, no browser needs an import.

  • The first time: export the passwords from your browser as a CSV file. Import the file in Nuvori for Android, then delete it. Sync takes the logins to your PC. Nuvori for Windows can also open a KeePass database.
  • Every browser after that: add the extension. The extension has no vault of its own, so there is nothing to copy.
  • Passkeys: the extension doesn't handle passkeys. Passkeys in Nuvori stay in your vault. Android 14 or newer and Windows 11 (Microsoft Store version) offer them in the system passkey prompt.

The extension can also turn off the browser's own “Save password?” offer, so only Nuvori asks. Turn it on in the extension's Settings.

Which browsers work?

Browsers built on Chromium or Firefox, on Windows, with Nuvori for Windows on the same PC.

  • Built on Chromium (version 127 or newer): Helium, Comet, Arc, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera, Thorium, Chromium, Chrome and Edge. Get the extension from the Chrome Web Store. In Edge, allow extensions from other stores first.
  • Built on Firefox (version 140 or newer): Zen, Firefox, LibreWolf, Waterfox, Floorp, Mullvad Browser and Tor Browser. Get the add-on from Firefox Add-ons. In these browsers, cards and addresses fill from the Nuvori button in the toolbar.

Safari doesn't work, and there is no Nuvori for Mac, iPhone, iPad or Linux. On Android, Nuvori fills through Android's autofill service instead of an extension.

Can I use Nuvori with a new browser like Zen, Helium or Comet?

Yes. Nuvori for Windows recognizes Zen, Helium, Comet, Arc and many more by name, and connects them by itself.

Helium has no built-in password manager, by design. Nuvori fills that gap.

A browser that Nuvori doesn't recognize yet can still work if it is built on Chromium or Firefox:

  1. Install the extension from the matching store.
  2. In Nuvori, open Settings > Browser extension.
  3. Next to the browser, choose Built on: Chromium or Built on: Firefox. Then turn on its switch.
  4. Restart the browser.

This works for most browsers built on Chromium or Firefox. A browser that reads its own, different connection key can't be connected this way.

Sync and devices

How do I add another phone or tablet?

Put both devices on the same Wi-Fi and keep them unlocked during pairing.

  1. On the new device, install Nuvori. Choose Join an existing vault and create the vault with the same master password as your other phone. Tap Create and continue.
  2. On the managing device, open Settings > Devices & sync and tap Create QR. Enter your master password and tap Verify and connect.
  3. On the new device, tap Scan QR and point the camera at the code. Enter its master password and tap Verify and pair.
  4. Check that both screens show the same code. Tap Codes match.
  5. Wait while the vault copies. Then tap Done.

The new device needs an empty vault. A device that already has items can't join this way.

How do I add a Windows PC, or a second PC?

Your phone adds PCs. A PC can't add other devices. One vault can have up to 2 PCs.

  1. Install Nuvori for Windows. Choose a master password and select Create vault.
  2. On the PC, open Devices & sync. Under Pair with your phone, keep Scan phone's QR selected.
  3. On the managing phone, open Settings > Devices & sync and tap Create QR. Enter your master password and tap Verify and connect.
  4. On the PC, select Use camera and hold the phone in front of it. No camera? Use Read QR image with a photo of the QR, or paste the link from Copy link into Setup link.
  5. Type your phone's master password in Phone's master password and select Continue.
  6. Check that both screens show the same code. Select Codes match on both devices.

Best: pair a PC with an empty vault. If the PC already has items, it keeps them and adds them to your vault as new copies. Items that are also on your phone then show twice.

The first pairing always needs both devices on the same network, for example the same Wi-Fi or a phone hotspot.

If the PC can't reach the phone, choose Show QR to phone on the PC. Then, on the phone, tap Scan Windows QR if the PC cannot reach this phone.

Should I sync over Wi-Fi or through Google Drive?

You can use both. A sync tries your devices on the same Wi-Fi first, then Google Drive.

  • Wi-Fi sync works as soon as devices are paired. They talk directly to each other on your local network.
  • Google Drive sync is optional. It helps when your devices are not on the same Wi-Fi, for example a phone on mobile data and a PC at home. Every change is encrypted before it goes to your Drive.

The first pairing always needs the same Wi-Fi. Google Drive only carries changes between devices that are already paired.

How do I turn on Google Drive sync?

Pair your devices first. The Google Drive card shows only after pairing. Use the same Google account on every device.

  1. On the phone, open Settings > Devices & sync. Find Sync when your devices are apart and tap Connect Google Drive. Choose your Google account and allow access.
  2. On the PC, open Devices & sync. On the same card, select Connect Google Drive. Sign in with the same account in your browser.
  3. When the browser says Google Drive is connected, close the tab and go back to Nuvori.

Nuvori gets access only to its own hidden folder in your Drive. It can't see or change your other files.

Does sync work when Nuvori is closed or locked?
  • Android over Wi-Fi: yes, while Automatic sync is on. Android shows a small notification while it runs. A locked phone receives changes and applies them after you unlock it.
  • Android through Google Drive: only while Nuvori is open, when you unlock it, and when you tap Sync now. Your own changes upload right after you make them.
  • Windows: only while the vault on the PC is unlocked.

Both apps can also check once each time you open them. That is the Sync when Nuvori opens switch.

One of my devices was off for weeks. Is that a problem?

No. Each device keeps its own copy and works offline. When it connects again, it gets every change it missed and sends its own.

Nuvori doesn't remove a device just because it was away.

  • Open Nuvori on it and unlock it, so it can sync.
  • If it missed app updates, update Nuvori first. See Do all my devices need the same Nuvori version?
  • If the same item changed on both devices meanwhile, Nuvori asks which version to keep. See What if I change the same item on two devices?
What if I change the same item on two devices?

If two devices change the same item before they sync with each other, Nuvori keeps both versions and asks you to choose. It doesn't quietly throw one away.

  • Phone: open Settings > Devices & sync. Under Conflicts to review, tap Review. Choose Keep this device's version or Use incoming version.
  • PC: open Devices & sync and select Review. Choose Keep this PC's version or Keep phone version.

Your choice syncs to your other devices. Colors and website icons never ask: the newest change wins.

Home says “Devices haven't synced in 3 days”. What should I do?

Your phone shows this when it hasn't exchanged changes with your other devices for more than 3 days. Your items are safe. Each device still has its own copy.

  1. Open Nuvori on your other device and unlock it.
  2. Put both devices on the same Wi-Fi, or turn on Google Drive sync on both.
  3. Tap Sync now.

The warning counts Google Drive syncs too. Older versions looked only at Wi-Fi sync, so update Nuvori on both devices if the warning stays.

Do all my devices need the same Nuvori version?

Your items sync between different versions. But some newer features start only when every device in the group has a version that knows them. For example:

  • Website icons that your PC shares with your phone.
  • Shared accent and background colors.
  • Removing a device so the others still get its last changes.

Until every device is updated, Nuvori waits with these changes. Nothing is lost.

If a device says changes could not be applied or could not be read, update Nuvori on all your devices. Then tap Retry changes on the phone. The easiest rule: keep every device up to date.

What is the managing device, and how do I move that role?

One phone or tablet in each group is the managing device. It adds new devices and removes old ones. Only an Android phone or tablet can manage the group, so the role can't move to a PC.

The managing device doesn't need to be online for everyday sync. Your other devices keep syncing with each other.

To move the role, for example to a new phone:

  1. Sync both devices.
  2. On the managing device, open Settings > Devices & sync. Under Managing role, tap Offer to and the other device's name. Tap Offer.
  3. On the other device, tap Accept managing role, then Accept.
  4. Back on the old managing device, tap Complete transfer.

Lost or new device

I lost my only phone. Can I get my vault back?

Only from a Nuvori backup. Your vault was only on that phone, and Nuvori has no server copy. Android's own Google backup doesn't include Nuvori.

If Google Drive backup was on:

  1. Install Nuvori on the new phone. Choose Restore a backup.
  2. Create a master password for this phone.
  3. Tap Find backups in Google Drive. Sign in with the Google account of your old phone.
  4. Pick the backup. Enter the old phone's master password in Master password of the backup.
  5. Check what Nuvori found, and confirm to replace the new, empty vault.

If you saved a .pvault file instead, tap Restore encrypted backup and choose the file.

Without a backup or another synced device, the vault can't be recovered. Google Drive backup is off until you turn it on, so turn it on today. See How do I make a backup?

I lost my phone, but my PC has my vault. What now?

Your PC has every change that reached it before the phone was lost. Only the lost phone could add or remove devices, so the old group can't be repaired. Instead, you start a new group from the new phone, and the PC moves into it.

  1. On the new phone, install Nuvori and create a new, empty vault. Don't restore a backup on it.
  2. On the PC, open Devices & sync and select Leave group. Choose Keep this PC's items, then Leave group.
  3. On the same Wi-Fi, pair the PC with the new phone, as in How do I add a Windows PC? On the PC, type the new phone's master password.
  4. The PC adds its items to the new group, and they copy to the new phone. Keep both unlocked until nothing is waiting.

The new group has new keys. The lost phone can't read anything you add from now on.

Changes that were only on the lost phone are gone. Already restored a backup on the new phone? Then choose Start with an empty vault on the PC in step 2. With Keep this PC's items, every item would show twice. Edits made only on the PC after that backup are then lost.

If the lost phone used Google Drive sync, also remove Nuvori's access in your Google account. See How do I stop a lost or stolen device from getting new data?

I got a new phone and my old one still works. How do I switch?
  1. Add the new phone to your vault, as in How do I add another phone or tablet?
  2. Wait until the new phone shows all your items, and the old phone shows nothing waiting.
  3. If the old phone is the managing device, move the role to the new phone: Offer to the new phone, Accept managing role there, then Complete transfer on the old phone. See What is the managing device?
  4. On the old phone, open Settings > Devices & sync. Under Leave the group, tap Remove this device from the group, then Leave group. Or, on the new phone, tap Remove and the old phone's name on its card.
  5. If you used them, turn on Google Drive sync and Google Drive backup on the new phone.
  6. Uninstall Nuvori from the old phone before you reset it or give it away.

If you use a watch for codes, set it up again from the new phone under Settings > Watch codes.

I reinstalled Windows or got a new PC. How do I set it up again?

Your old PC is still listed in your group, and it counts toward the limit of 2 PCs. Remove it first.

  1. On the managing phone, open Settings > Devices & sync. On the old PC's card, tap Remove and its name, then Remove.
  2. Install Nuvori for Windows on the new PC and select Create vault.
  3. Pair it with your phone, as in How do I add a Windows PC?
  4. Install the browser extension again in each browser you use.

The PC receives an encrypted copy of your vault from the phone. Changes that were only on the old PC, and never synced, are not in that copy.

Nuvori for Windows has no backup of its own. Your phone is where the vault comes back from.

I have three devices and lost one. What should I do?

Your other devices keep their copies and keep syncing with each other. The next step depends on which device you lost.

You lost a device that is not the managing device:

  1. On the managing phone, open Settings > Devices & sync.
  2. On the lost device's card, tap Remove and its name.
  3. If the dialog names another of your devices, cancel and sync that device first. Then it gets every change the lost device made. Tap Remove to finish.

You lost the managing device: the others still sync, but no device can add or remove devices, and the old group can't be repaired. Start a new group from a phone or tablet you keep:

  1. Choose the phone or tablet with the newest items. Sync all your devices first, until nothing is waiting.
  2. On that device, open Settings > Devices & sync. Tap Remove this device from the group, then Leave group. It keeps its items, gets new keys, and can now add devices.
  3. On your PC, select Leave group and choose Start with an empty vault. Then pair it with the device from step 2.
  4. Another phone or tablet must join with an empty vault. Clear Nuvori's storage in Android settings, or reinstall Nuvori, then pair it.

Changes that reach only the old group after step 2 are not copied over. If you have only a PC left, see I lost my phone, but my PC has my vault.

How do I stop a lost or stolen device from getting new data?

Remove it from your sync group on the managing phone. The lost device doesn't need to agree.

  1. Open Settings > Devices & sync.
  2. On the lost device's card, tap Remove and its name. Tap Remove to confirm.

Your other devices learn about it at their next sync, over Wi-Fi or Google Drive. Then they refuse its changes and stop syncing with it.

Removal doesn't change your vault's encryption keys. So if the lost device used Google Drive sync, also do this:

  1. Remove Nuvori's access in your Google account's security settings.
  2. Select Sign in again on each device you keep.

For the strongest protection, start a new group, which gets new keys. See How do I leave a sync group or start a new one? If you lost the managing phone, you can't remove it. See I have three devices and lost one.

Removal can't erase what is already on the lost device. That copy stays encrypted, and the vault locks when the screen turns off. If you think someone knows your master password, change the passwords of your most important accounts.

How do I leave a sync group or start a new one?
  • A phone or tablet: open Settings > Devices & sync. Under Leave the group, tap Remove this device from the group, then Leave group. It keeps its items as a separate vault.
  • The managing device: move the role first. See What is the managing device? Or tap Start a separate group on this device instead. The other devices keep their copies, but no one can add or remove devices in the old group.
  • A PC: open Devices & sync and select Leave group. Choose Keep this PC's items or Start with an empty vault.

A phone or tablet that left starts its own group with new keys. Pair new devices with it as usual. If you used a watch, pair it again for codes.

Devices that join the new group must start empty. On a PC, choose Start with an empty vault when it leaves. On a phone, clear Nuvori's storage or reinstall it. Your old sync files stay in Google Drive until you delete them.

Sync every device first, and resolve conflicts. A phone can't leave while changes are still waiting. A device can't rejoin a group it left as it is: a PC needs Reset this PC's vault in Settings first, and a phone needs a fresh install.

Backup and restore

How do I make a backup?

You make backups on your Android phone. There are two kinds, and you can use both. Google Drive backup is off until you turn it on. We recommend it, especially if Nuvori is only on one phone.

Google Drive backup (automatic):

  1. Open Settings > Backup and import.
  2. Under Google Drive backup, choose how often in Back up every.
  3. Tap Turn on Google Drive backup and choose your Google account.
  4. Enter your master password and tap Turn on.

Backups run while Nuvori is unlocked. Back up now makes one right away.

Backup file (manual):

  1. Open Settings > Backup and import.
  2. Under Encrypted backup, tap Export encrypted backup.
  3. Enter your master password and choose where to save the .pvault file.

Both kinds include your items, photos and passkeys, and open only with your master password. Nuvori for Windows has no backup feature. It gets your vault by pairing with your phone.

Where should I keep my backup? Isn't sync enough?

Sync is not a backup. If you delete an item on one device, sync deletes it on the others too. A backup is a copy from one moment.

Good places for a .pvault file:

  • A USB stick or external drive that you keep at home.
  • A cloud folder you trust. The file is encrypted, but anyone with the file and your master password can open it.
  • More than one place. Make a new file after big changes.

Google Drive backup keeps itself up to date. Nuvori keeps this phone's newest backup and the one before it, in a hidden Nuvori folder.

A backup always needs the master password from when it was made. If you change your master password later, older backup files still need the old one.

How do I restore a backup?

A restore replaces the whole vault on this phone. Nuvori checks the backup and shows what it contains before it replaces anything.

  1. Open Settings > Backup and import. On a new phone, you can also choose Restore a backup during setup.
  2. For Google Drive, tap Find backups in Google Drive and pick a backup. For a file, tap Restore encrypted backup and choose the .pvault file.
  3. Enter the master password that was in use when the backup was made.
  4. Check the summary and confirm.
  5. Unlock Nuvori again with this phone's master password.

Then set up fingerprint unlock again, and pair your other devices again if you use sync. Restore works only on Android.

Can I restore a backup on a device that is already in a sync group?

You can, but it takes that device out of the group without telling the others. The restored vault becomes a separate vault and stops syncing. Your other devices keep their own copies, don't get the restored items, and still list the device until the managing phone removes it. If you restore on the managing phone, the old group has no managing device anymore.

On a synced device you usually don't need a restore:

  • To get back a deleted item, use Recently deleted. See I deleted an item by mistake.
  • To fix one device, remove it from the group and pair it again as an empty device. It gets a fresh copy from the others.

If the whole group needs the backup's contents, restore it on one phone. Then start the group again from that phone: the other devices join it with empty vaults.

I deleted an item by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes, for 30 days.

  1. Open Settings > Recently deleted on your phone or PC.
  2. Find the item and select Restore.

On your phone, items deleted on another device show up here too. A restored item syncs to your other devices again. Photos and passkeys are not kept here.

Need an older password of a login? Open the login on your phone and look under Previous passwords. It keeps the last 10.

Autofill and passkeys

How do I turn on autofill on Android?
  1. Open Settings > Autofill and codes.
  2. If it says Nuvori is not your Autofill service, tap Choose Nuvori autofill and select Nuvori.
  3. On Android 14 or newer, also tap Enable passwords and passkeys and turn on Nuvori.
  4. For Chrome or Brave, tap Open browser autofill settings. Turn on autofill using another service, then restart the browser.

Nuvori offers a login only on the website or app it is saved for, and fills websites only over HTTPS.

Can my logins show in the keyboard instead of a popup?

Yes, it's a setting. Open Settings > Autofill and codes and find How logins appear:

  • Account picker (the default): after you unlock, Nuvori shows the logins for this app or site right away. Tap one to fill it, or search all your logins.
  • Keyboard suggestions: after you unlock, your logins appear in the keyboard. Tap the field again if the keyboard closed. Choose another login opens the account picker.

Codes and new passwords always use the picker. Keyboards without inline suggestions show Android's suggestion menu instead.

What is the Quick copy tile?

A Quick Settings tile on Android. It copies a password, code, card detail or security answer when autofill can't help. It works even if another app is your autofill service.

  1. Open Settings > Autofill and codes and tap Add Quick copy tile.
  2. Pull down Quick Settings, tap Quick copy and unlock.
  3. Choose Passwords, TOTP, Cards or Questions. Tap a field to copy it.

The other fields of that item stay in your notifications for 2 minutes, so you can paste them one by one.

How does the browser extension fill on my PC?

The extension, Nuvori Password Fill, has no vault of its own. It asks Nuvori for Windows for the login you choose.

  1. Install Nuvori for Windows and unlock it.
  2. Install the extension from the Chrome Web Store or Firefox Add-ons.
  3. Open a sign-in page. Pick your login under the field, or click the Nuvori icon in the browser toolbar.

When you sign in with a new login, Nuvori offers to save it. Logins you save on the PC sync to your phone.

What does the passkey manager show?

On your phone, open Settings > Passkeys. It has three parts:

  • Saved passkeys: every passkey, with search. Tap one to see its site, account, when it was added, the app that created it, the last change, how often you used it, its public key fingerprint, and short IDs.
  • Passkey history: when passkeys were added, synced, used or deleted. Changes only hides sign-ins. The history stays on this phone.
  • Passkey requests: the recent apps and websites that asked Nuvori, and why Nuvori was not offered, if it wasn't.

A passkey's private key is never shown. It stays encrypted in your vault.

Do my passkeys sync and get backed up?

Yes. Passkeys are stored encrypted in your vault. They sync to your other devices and are included in backups. Passkey history and the request list stay on each device.

  • On Android 14 or newer, Nuvori can create and use passkeys in apps and websites.
  • On Windows 11, turn on Passkeys in Windows in Nuvori's settings.
  • To sign in on a computer with a passkey from your phone, choose the computer's option to use a phone. Scan the QR code with your phone, tap Unlock Nuvori and pick the passkey.

Website icons

Where do the website icons come from?

From two places. Nuvori never asks an icon service, so no icon service learns which sites you use.

  • Built-in logos: both apps include logos for about 800 popular sites.
  • Icons from your browser: when you use a site that already has a login or passkey in Nuvori, the browser extension on your PC takes that site's own icon and sends a small copy to Nuvori for Windows. The browser usually has the icon in its cache already. If not, the extension fetches it from the address the page gives, without cookies. Sync then shares it with your other devices.

Sites without an icon show their first letter.

To turn icons off, use Settings > Appearance > Website icons on the phone, or Settings > Website icons on the PC.

Why doesn't my phone show the icons my PC shows?

Your phone never saves icons from websites itself. It shows the built-in logos and the icons your PC shares through sync. Check these:

  • Both apps are up to date. The PC shares icons only when every device in your group has a version that supports them. Until then, it waits.
  • The devices synced after you used the site on the PC.
  • Website icons is on in the phone's Appearance settings.
A website changed its logo. When does Nuvori update it?

Your PC asks the browser for a saved site's icon again after 30 days. The next time you use that site, the extension sends the new icon, and sync passes it on. The newest icon wins on every device.

Built-in logos change only with an app update.

To refresh all icons now, select Remove saved icons under Settings > Website icons on the PC. Each site gets its icon again the next time you use it.

Storage and logs

Does sync history keep growing forever?

Partly, yes. To sync safely, each device keeps a signed record of the changes in your vault. Nuvori doesn't clean up old records yet, so this record grows a little with each change.

Other lists have limits:

  • Recently deleted: 30 days.
  • Previous passwords: the last 10 per login.
  • Passkey history: at most 3,000 entries. Passkey requests: the last 100.
  • Activity log on the PC: at most 500 events, kept only until Nuvori quits.
  • Website icons saved on the PC: at most 2,000.

Google Drive sync files also stay in your Drive. See How much Google Drive space does Nuvori use?

How do I clear recent activity and logs?
  • Activity log on the PC: open Settings > Activity log, select Clear, then Clear log.
  • Passkey history on the phone: open Settings > Passkeys and tap Clear history. Your passkeys stay saved.
  • Passkey requests on the phone: tap Clear request history on the same page.
  • Recently deleted: select Empty Recently deleted on the phone or PC.
  • Previous passwords: open the login and select Clear under Previous passwords.

These lists never contain your passwords. Clearing them doesn't change your items or your sync. The signed sync record can't be cleared by hand.

How much Google Drive space does Nuvori use?

Nuvori uses a hidden app folder in your own Drive. It counts toward your Google storage.

  • Sync: encrypted changes and photos. Nuvori doesn't delete old sync files yet, so the folder grows slowly.
  • Backup: each phone keeps its newest backup and the one before it.

When your Drive is full, Nuvori says so on the Google Drive card. Free some space, then sync again.

To delete everything, open Google Drive in a web browser, go to its settings, then the page that manages apps. Find Nuvori and delete its hidden app data. Do this only when no device uses Google Drive sync or backup anymore. Otherwise, the next sync uploads the files again.

Troubleshooting

Sync seems stuck. What can I try?
  1. Unlock Nuvori on both devices. A PC syncs only while it is unlocked.
  2. Put both devices on the same Wi-Fi. Guest networks and some routers stop devices from reaching each other. A phone hotspot often works.
  3. Tap Sync now on both.
  4. If a device isn't found, enter its address by hand. On the phone, tap Show connection details, then Connection help on the device card, and enter the other device's Wi-Fi IP address. On the PC, use Optional device address under Connection, then Save and sync.
  5. If Nuvori says changes could not be applied or could not be read, update Nuvori on every device. Then tap Retry changes.
  6. Check Conflicts to review. Nuvori waits for your choice on items changed on two devices.

Still stuck? The PC's Activity log shows recent sync messages. Tell us what happened.

A device says “Not seen yet” or “Not checked yet”.

This device hasn't finished a sync with that device yet. The other device may be off, locked, or on another network.

  • Open Nuvori on that device and unlock it.

If the device is gone for good, remove it. See How do I stop a lost or stolen device from getting new data?

The browser extension doesn't fill my login.
  1. Start Nuvori for Windows and unlock it. The extension can't fill while the vault is locked.
  2. Click the Nuvori icon in the toolbar. If it says Not connected, follow the steps it shows.
  3. In Nuvori, open Settings > Browser extension. Make sure your browser is listed and Connected. If not, select Reconnect all and restart the browser.
  4. Check the address bar. Nuvori fills passwords only on HTTPS sites.
  5. Open the login in Nuvori and check that this site's address is saved in it.
  6. No suggestions under the field? In the extension, check Suggest logins in pages, and that the site is not under Don't suggest on.
  7. Still nothing? Select Test connection under Diagnostics in the extension.
Nuvori doesn't offer my login in an Android app or website.
  • Check that Nuvori is your autofill service in Settings > Autofill and codes.
  • In Chrome or Brave, turn on autofill using another service, then restart the browser.
  • Open the login and check its saved website. A different subdomain needs its own website.
  • For an app, edit the login and add the app under Autofill apps.
  • HTTP pages, unverified browsers and confusing forms are not filled. Some apps block autofill completely.
  • When autofill can't help, copy from the Quick copy tile.

Passkey requests under Settings > Passkeys shows whether an app asked Nuvori at all.

Privacy and security

Can Nuvori or the developer see my passwords?

No. Your vault is encrypted on each of your devices. There is no Nuvori account and no Nuvori server, so there is nowhere to send it.

  • Wi-Fi sync goes straight between your devices, encrypted.
  • Google Drive sync and backup store only encrypted files in your own Drive.
  • The apps and the extension collect no analytics, crash reports or advertising IDs.

Nuvori has not had an independent security audit. The privacy policy has all the details.

What can Google see when I use Google Drive?

Google can't read your items. Every change and photo is encrypted on your device before it is uploaded. Nuvori asks only for its own hidden app folder, and can't see your other Drive files.

Google can see that your account uses Nuvori, how many files there are, their sizes, and when they were written. File names use short codes, not your device names or websites.

Google could delete or hold back files. That would delay sync, but it can't change your items: every device checks the signature on each change.
