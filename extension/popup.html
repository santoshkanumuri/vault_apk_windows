        Browser password manager

        Passwords already saved in the browser still show up in its own suggestions. Extensions can't hide them. Turning this off, or removing Nuvori, gives these settings back to the browser.

        Move your passwords to Nuvori
        1. Export them from chrome://password-manager/settings.
        2. Import the file into Nuvori on your phone, then delete the file.
        3. Delete the passwords from the browser so its suggestions stop.
